## Listing description (long)

Stag is built for brands whose products deserve a slower look — premium
footwear, leather goods, apparel, and considered objects. A warm, editorial
art direction pairs a confident sans-serif with an accent serif, on a
palette of cream, sand, and deep forest.

The product page is the centerpiece: a sticky, cross-fading gallery whose
media follows the selected color, availability-aware variant pills, a size
chart dialog, and a sticky add-to-bag bar on mobile. The card-based cart
drawer keeps customers moving with a free-shipping progress banner, gift
options, order notes, and product recommendations.

Merchants tell their story with editorial sections — image with text and
stats, video reels, testimonials, a review carousel, gallery grids, and a
countdown for real campaigns. Every section ships with quality placeholder
photography, so the theme looks finished from the first minute in the
editor.

**Highlights**

- Color-aware product gallery — media tied to a color shows only when that
  color is selected
- Card-based cart drawer with free-shipping progress, gift message, notes,
  and recommendations
- Featured product section with live variant, price, and image updates
- Faceted filtering, predictive search, quick browsing across 15+ sections
- Scroll-reveal motion system that fully respects reduced-motion settings
- Every key flow works without JavaScript
