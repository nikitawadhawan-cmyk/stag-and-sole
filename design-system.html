Stag & Sole
Design System v1.0

The single source of truth for every Stag & Sole page. Flat, warm, editorial. If a decision isn't covered here, the answer is almost always: less ornament, more whitespace, ink on cream.

01

Color

Five brand colors plus three text/line utilities. Cream is the only page background. Sand is the only surface for media and cards. Green and ink are the only dark surfaces. Tan is never a background — it lives in hovers, links, stars, and small accents only.

02

Typography

Two typefaces, one rule. Archivo (400/500/600) does all the work. Instrument Serif italic appears only as a single accent word or short phrase inside a heading — never for whole headings, never for body text, never roman. In Shopify the serif maps to the accent font_picker (default: Playfair Display).

Cards & content blocks

Cards are flat: sand or media on a 4px radius, meta text below, no borders, no shadows. Media always object-fit cover on a sand backdrop. Overlay text uses an ink gradient scrim (transparent → 60% ink).

06

Logo & ornament

The wordmark is stacked: word / ruled ampersand row / word. The serif italic ampersand is green on light, tan on dark. The ruled-line motif (thin line · serif glyph · thin line) is the brand's only ornament — no icons, no illustrations. Where an icon feels needed, use a text label or a 5px tan dot instead.

07

Layout, imagery, motion, voice

Layout
  • Container 1320px, gutters 40px (24px ≤1024px)
  • Section rhythm: 76–80px top padding (56px ≤700px)
  • Grid gaps: 18px cards, 16px tiles, 10px dense (UGC)
  • Breakpoints: 1180 / 1024 / 900 / 700
  • Radii: 2px tags · 3px controls · 4px cards. Never larger.
  • No shadows anywhere. Depth = color, scrims, hairlines.
Imagery
  • Warm, sunlit, editorial product photography
  • Always on sand backdrop, object-fit cover
  • Ratios: hero 3:4 · PDP 4:5 · reels 9:16 · UGC 1:1
  • Shoes in use — streets, cafes, commutes; not studio-sterile
  • Ink gradient scrim under any text over imagery
Motion
  • Color/background transitions only, 0.15–0.18s ease
  • No movement, scale, parallax, or entrance animations
  • Smooth scroll for anchors; respect prefers-reduced-motion
Voice
  • Sentence case everywhere except the wordmark and eyebrows
  • Honest, specific, lightly wry: "Wear them home first"
  • Prices stated plainly; the value story is direct-from-workshop, never "discount"
  • ₹ with Indian grouping: ₹3,499 · ₹6,000
  • Do: "42 hand steps per pair"   Don't: "Premium luxury quality"
