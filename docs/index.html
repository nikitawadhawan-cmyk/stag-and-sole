Setup

Getting started

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes and add Stag, then click Customize to open the theme editor.
  2. Set your brand under Theme settings → Colors and Typography. Stag ships with a warm cream / forest palette and pairs a sans-serif with an accent serif — both fonts are pickable.
  3. Upload your logo in the header section, or leave it empty to use the stacked text wordmark.
  4. Assign your menus (see Navigation), then work through the homepage sections top to bottom.
Tip: every section that shows imagery has a placeholder image picker, so the theme looks finished while you're still gathering photography. Uploaded images always take priority.

PDP

Product pages

  • Gallery: sticky, cross-fading media with a thumbnail rail. Media assigned to a color variant only shows when that color is selected.
  • Variant picker: availability-aware pills; sold-out combinations are struck through. A size-chart dialog is available on the size option.
  • Blocks: the product info column is fully block-based — eyebrow, title, price, picker, buy buttons, accordions, share icons, custom Liquid, and app blocks can be reordered freely.
  • Share: direct share icons for Facebook, X, Pinterest, and WhatsApp, plus copy-link.
  • Recommendations: "You may also like" and "Seen on feet" sections come configured on the default template.
Cart

Cart drawer & cart page

The slide-out cart is card-based and fully configurable under Theme settings → Cart:

  • Offers bar with milestones: set a free-shipping threshold plus up to two reward milestones (e.g. free gift wrap). The drawer shows a "Pick your gifts" progress banner that expands to a milestone tracker.
  • Coupon field: customers can enter a discount code in the drawer; valid codes celebrate with confetti and show the reduced total.
  • Gift option: a gift checkbox and message, saved onto the order as cart attributes.
  • Order note, login card, product recommendations: each can be shown or hidden independently.
Note: discount codes are created in your Shopify admin under Discounts. The theme applies whatever codes your store offers — it never invents discounts.
Menus

Navigation

  • Main menu: supports three levels; second-level items render as dropdown columns, and a mega-menu option with a promo image is available in header settings.
  • Footer: up to four columns — three link columns (each takes any menu) and a newsletter block with social icons.
  • Utility bar: an optional top strip with its own small menu, social icons, and the country and language selectors.
  • Menu items with # as their link act as headings: the header renders their children as a dropdown, and the footer lists the children directly.
Catalog

Collections & filters

  • Faceted filtering (availability, price, options) uses Shopify's native filters. Configure them once in the free Search & Discovery app under Apps → Search & Discovery → Filters — until then, only sorting appears.
  • Products per page, image hover behavior, and quick-view are collection section settings.
  • The search page supports the same filters, plus a predictive search overlay in the header.
Global

Localization

  • Country/currency selector appears automatically in the utility bar when more than one market country is active.
  • Language selector appears when your store publishes more than one language (Settings → Languages).
  • All theme copy is translatable via Edit default theme content.
  • Image focal points set in the Shopify admin are respected across the theme's cropped imagery.
Help

FAQ

My collection pages show no filters.

Install and configure the free Search & Discovery app (see Collections & filters).

How do I show color swatches instead of text pills?

Connect your color option to Shopify's taxonomy (edit the option in the admin and pick the standard Color attribute). The theme renders swatch colors and images automatically once linked.

The countdown section shows nothing.

It requires a real end date — set one in the section settings. It never fakes urgency.

How do I change what opens when customers add to cart?

Theme settings → Cart → Open cart drawer when adding to cart. Turned off, the buttons link to the cart page.

Something else?

Use the support form — include your store URL and theme version and we'll reply within two business days.
